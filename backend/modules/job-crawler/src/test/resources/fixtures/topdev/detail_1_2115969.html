Supercent, a global gaming company with over 1.6 billion downloads worldwide in record time, is officially launching its new journey in Vietnam. 

 

We are looking for talented individuals to join us in building globally successful titles. Founded in 2021, Supercent is currently ranked as the 5th top game publisher worldwide, and is recognized as one of the fastest-growing publishers in the global market. 

 

Hit titles such as Pizza Ready, Burger Please!, and Snake Clash have attracted hundreds of millions of users around the world, consistently topping global app store charts and earning strong user acclaim. 

1
Your role & responsibilities
  • Design and create UI resources for mobile games. 
  • Plan UI direction and presentation considering the characteristics of each game. 
  • Design intuitive layouts prioritizing user experience (UX). 
  • Collaborate with planners, developers, and related departments to implement optimized UI. 
  • Provide flexible design solutions suitable for various game genres. 
2
Your skills & qualifications
  •  At least 5 years of practical experience in game UI production. 
  • Experience servicing live games. 
  • Deep understanding of system UI and monetization UI structures. 
  • Able to design UI presentation and UX-based structures. 
  • Proficient in graphic tools such as Photoshop and Illustrator. 
  • Able to flexibly adapt to various game genre styles.  

 

Required Skills 

  • Adobe Photoshop (Required) 
  • Adobe Illustrator (Required) 
  • After Effects (Preferred) 
  • Adobe XD (Preferred) 
  • Figma (Preferred) 
  • Understanding of Unity UI systems  

 

Nice to have 

  • Deep understanding of casual games and various other genres. 
  • Experience with Unity-based UI presentation or prototype production. 
  • Able to create UI motion work using After Effects. 
  • Experience using UX prototyping tools such as Adobe XD and Figma. 
  • Strong understanding of game planning and development processes.
